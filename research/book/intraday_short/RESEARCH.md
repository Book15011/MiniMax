# Research to direct the finite pool
Read October10 2026. Primary sources only. Existing frozen macro checkpoint is not refreshed by these mechanism papers.

- [Shen, Urquhart, Wang, Financial Review2022](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/fire.12290): volume-defined first half-hour predicts last half-hour; stronger high-volume/high-volatility conditions and downturn economic results. This supports exploring state/volume-conditioned continuation. It does not validate a fifteen-minute threshold, altcoin universe, our costs or current direction.
- [Kitron, Wengrowicz2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21888): fifteen-minute sign reversal, stronger after aggressive taker flow, but reported gross edge around1.3bp below5bp roundtrip benchmark. Unreviewed. Competition market fees alone total about20bp. Therefore do not trade every candle reversal; test failed rebound/large bounce in a weak broader regime, with a move-size filter. That filter is our hypothesis, not this paper's demonstrated result.
- [Binance public-data specification](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data): daily/monthly klines expose completed OHLCV, quote volume and taker-buy quote volume, with2025+spot timestamps in microseconds and checksum files. Taker imbalance is a bar-level tape measure, not order-book depth or Roostoo execution liquidity.
- [Roostoo public API](https://github.com/roostoo/Roostoo-API-Documents): public ticker can return all listed pairs in one request. Twenty calls/min is a ceiling for our process, not a reason to force20trades. Shared account30calls/min still includesqueries. Offline tests need no signed or live competition requests.
- [Organizer scoring](https://luma.com/coghwiyt): portfolio return qualification precedes0.4Sortino+0.3Sharpe+0.3Calmar. A high hit rate with poor net returns does not solve the competition problem. Follow repo directional-only/noHFT rules.

Mechanisms deliberately differ: continuation after range break; entry after failed countertrend bounce; exhaustion after a large bounce; cross-sectional laggard confirmation; taker-flow confirmation of breakout. No blind parameter grid. A stop is a monitored trigger with delayed market execution, so rebound gaps can cost more than its threshold.

Recent accepted prefix demonstrates reduced long exposure helped losses, while extra turnover impaired broader history. Thus evaluate the short-only sleeve independently before proposing any combination with long models. All current data is already seen; subsequent narrowing must disclose reuse and retain every initial candidate.
