# Selective bearish entries and rebound exits

Four frozen hypotheses: a low-exposure fast-trend control; a BTC/breadth bearish gate with rebound exit; the same gate requiring a three-hour pullback then one-hour rejection; and the same gate requiring a fresh twelve-hour close breakdown without an excessive extension. Parameters are fixed before looking at this study's results. All studies remain retrospective because these historical periods have been examined before. Four models do not imply four independent discoveries.

All four use only the same BTC/ETH/BNB/XRP intersection with point-in-time universe, hourly completed candles and 1h decisions, a 1h execution delay, 1pp band, at most25%gross and10%per coin, conservative daily risk1%, shared planner/PaperBroker and cash100k. All numeric tunables live in YAML. Registration reads only cadence/band from YAML; targets have no I/O. For selective variants, an inherited short may persist under a weaker trend threshold; a new short requires its own entry trigger. Existing target size may only shrink until flat, to avoid repeated additions and fees. This previous-target state is model state, not observed inventory. Rebound exits depend solely on known prices: above EMA12, trend reversal, BTC/breadth clear, or an excessive six-hour rebound. No protective fill at an unobserved intrabar high/low is assumed; every exit incurs the same delay.

The main supplemental dataset and lookalikes remain exactly the earlier69cases (68unique historic cases plus spent108hcompetition prefix),25 archive-weak and25 recent-BTC-weak checkpoints, horizons216/228/240h. All windows start from cash. Never select only future market falls. The new4and previous14 define18declared hypotheses for this report's simultaneous quarter-cluster lower bounds; earlier research choices remain an unquantified selection limitation. Main --score2 is unchanged, including daily activity guard, incumbent comparisons and original/stronger gates. Supplemental episode replay omits the activity guard; distinguish them.

Report actual completed profitable short cycles after fees/spread separately from onsets profitable after1/3/6h. Still-open episodes are censored from completed accuracy, included at full endpoint closing cost in return. A profitable paper episode does not prove a profitable live strategy. Require all unchanged stronger-evidence rules, not just higher accuracy: all six lower bounds positive,30completed shorts,12quarters,12firingwindows, doubled-cost mean positive, median nonnegative, positive-control return no worse than-1%. Never loosen gates or stop at a favourable subset. Failed trials are retained.

Evidence supporting this experiment is limited: the previous price portfolios made gains in still-open shorts on the early competition prefix, but completed shorts often lost and all six lost on the newly inspected October9day. That motivates testing entry selectivity and exits; it does not prove exits were the only problem. Historical AQR trend evidence uses monthly/yearly horizons, not hourly crypto (https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Time-Series-Momentum). Daniel/Moskowitz document momentum losses during rebounds in traditional assets (https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439); applying a rebound guard here is a hypothesis to test, not a validated transfer.

This changes no live bot or shared execution code. The clone and frozenOctober10predictions remain separate. BCHabsence29/30and disabled adaptive activation are preserved.
