# Sources and what they justify

Milan Fičura (2023), Impact of size and volume on cryptocurrency momentum and reversal.
FFA Working Paper3/2023, Prague University of Economics and Business.
https://quantitative.cz/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/impact_of_size_and_volume_on_cryptocurrency_momentum_and_reversal.pdf
Read abstract, construction and empirical sections. It motivates comparing distance from recent highs with ordinary
momentum. Effects differ with size/liquidity. This preliminary working paper does not validate our model, high proxy,
universe, horizon or expected competition return. Its market-cap/aggregate-volume filters are not available here.
We use inclusive hourly CLOSE maxima, not intraday OHLC highs. No source numerical return is treated as a backtest.

Wen, Bouri, Xu and Zhao (2022), Intraday return predictability in the cryptocurrency markets: Momentum, reversal, or both.
The North American Journal of Economics and Finance62,101733.
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1062940822000833
Publisher search preview/abstract available; full article not obtained. It cautions that reversal and continuation both
occur and can depend on conditions. No exact indicator, fitted coefficient or intraday strategy taken from this source.

Eight earlier recovery hypotheses failed. Selecting another mechanism is exploratory research on spent history.
All new parameters are frozen before outcomes. Passing software tests is separate from a profitable strategy.
