# Main-short rebound protection motivation

Daniel and Moskowitz2016, Momentum Crashes, Journal of Financial Economics122(2),221–247.
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X16301490
Author/publisher summary: https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Momentum-Crashes
Past losers can rebound strongly following downturns and damage strategies shorting them.
We test a fixed direction-confirmation guard and a broad-market rebound guard; these are our hypotheses,
not the source's dynamic forecasts. No market-direction certainty or empirical crypto edge is inferred.

Ten prior task variants failed. Repeating their parameter search would not create fresh evidence.
Our distinct scope is the main DOWN-mode trend shorts, explicitly excluded from the earlier cash-short24h filter.
Every rule and threshold is frozen before outcomes; no historically favorable coin/date is picked.
