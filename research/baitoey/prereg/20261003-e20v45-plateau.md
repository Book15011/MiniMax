# Pre-registration: robustness check of pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45 under scoring v2 (Baitoey, 2026-10-03)

Written and committed before any run. Not edited after results exist; results go to
`results/baitoey/20261003-e20v45-plateau/` and a report committed under `research/baitoey/results/`.

## Question

`pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45` (Pol) holds `baitoey_vt_mom` with a 4.5%/day volatility cap while BTC is in trend (a
20-day EMA of hourly closes with a 3% band), and `pol_trend_ls` (long/short trend) otherwise. It scores 0.568 (tool
`3036a0d8bfdaa6a0`) and is the best finalist in strong falls (36%) and in the real falling round (#2 of 47). Its
EMA length and cap were chosen after a sensitivity check (Pol, `reports/review/20261002-recheck.md`); none of its
settings has been nudged one at a time. `baitoey_vt_mom` turned out to be a peak (`20261002-vt-plateau`); is this
model a plateau?

## Runs

One setting changed at a time; everything else as in `config.yaml` (`models: pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45`).

| Run | Change | Role |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | none | base: the registered run (read from the registry) |
| E1 / E2 | switch EMA 15 / 25 days (base 20) | neighbours |
| E3 / E4 | switch band 2% / 4% (base 3%) | neighbours |
| E5 / E6 | momentum-sleeve cap 3.5% / 5.5% per day (base 4.5%) | neighbours |

Scored one at a time with `score_model` like `--score`, not registered (the leaderboard is untouched). Script:
`research/baitoey/e20v45_plateau.py`. Reference: `baitoey_vt_mom` (registered).

## Verdict (fixed now)

- **Score holds** if every neighbour passes the hard gates and has HEADLINE_RET >= E0 - 0.03 (the team's margin).
- **Falls edge holds** if every neighbour clears the bars in strong-fall windows at least 10 points more often than
  `baitoey_vt_mom` (its reason to exist).
- **Robust** if both hold. Otherwise **not robust**, naming the runs that fail.
- Report-only: HEADLINE_RET without the post-holdout windows, hit rates in strong / mild falls and rises and in
  volatile windows, weighted median and mean R_liq, worst R_liq, the two real-round replays, guard share, fees.

## Known limits, stated before running

- The EMA and cap were chosen after looking; the base itself is a post-hoc pick, so even a plateau does not make it
  pre-registered.
- The post-holdout windows (Jul-Sep 2026) are in the pool and were already seen.
- Six comparisons: one neighbour falling short by chance is possible; the report shows all of them.
- Shorts are not yet tested on the exchange; this check is about the backtest only.
- No setting is changed because of this check before the launch decision.
