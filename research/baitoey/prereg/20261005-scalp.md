## Question

On some Roostoo pairs the price step is larger than two maker fees (0.10%), and the spread is exactly one step
(snapshot 2026-09-28: BONK 0.285%, PEPE 0.237%, STO 0.228%, 1000CHEEMS 0.183%, SHIB 0.177%, WLFI 0.176%, HEMI 0.166%).
Does buying with a limit one step below the last price and selling one step above the fill beat e20v45?

## Rules (per coin, a separate sub-account; capital split equally over the eligible coins at each round start)

- Eligible at a round start: Roostoo PricePrecision step (snapshot 2026-09-28) / the 1-minute close >= 0.15%.
- At each 1-minute bar close, if flat with no resting buy: post a buy limit at (last-price level - 1 step), 90% of the
  sub-account. A resting buy older than 5 minutes is cancelled and reposted if the level has moved up (2 calls).
- Fills only from the next bar on. Buy fills if the bar's low trades below the limit (THROUGH, primary) or touches it
  (TOUCH, report-only); maker fee 0.05% in coin. The sell is posted at entry + 1 step at that bar's close and can fill
  from the next bar (high above it, THROUGH; or at it, TOUCH), maker fee 0.05%.
- Stop: if a bar's low reaches entry - 3 steps, market sell at the stop - 1 step (or the open - 1 step after a gap),
  taker fee 0.1%. If the stop and the target fall in the same bar, the stop counts first.
- Round return: liquidation value at the round end (close - 1 step, taker fee) over the value at the start.
- Calls: one per place, two per cancel-and-repost; peak and mean per minute over all candidate coins reported.
