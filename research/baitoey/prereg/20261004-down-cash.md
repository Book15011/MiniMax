Written and committed before any run. Not edited after results exist; results go to

## Question

In strong-fall rounds (BTC down 5% or more over 14 days) holding cash beats e20v45 in 66% of rounds, by a median
2.9% (`results/baitoey/20261004-who-beats-us.txt`, after looking). e20v45 runs `pol_trend_ls` (long/short trend) in
down mode. Would cash in down mode do better? Against it: the down mode made e20v45 #2 of 47 in the real falling round.

## Runs

`pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45` with the `pol_trend_ls` sleeve removed from its config (the switch then holds nothing in
down mode: cash, with the usual activity guard). Up mode, the 4.5% cap and the switch rule are unchanged.

| Run | Down mode | Switch EMA days | Same-EMA reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | long/short trend (registered e20v45) | 20 | — |
| DC20 | cash | 20 | E0 |
| DC15 / DC25 | cash | 15 / 25 | E1 / E2 of `20261003-e20v45-plateau` (0.565 / 0.577) |

Scored with `score_model` like `--score`, not registered (the leaderboard is untouched). Report-only: DC20 and E0
with the live lock-in, by the method of Pol's endgame2.py. Script: `research/baitoey/down_cash.py`.

## Verdict (fixed now)

- DC20 is **better** than E0 if it passes the hard gates and HEADLINE_RET >= E0 + 0.03, or HEADLINE_RET >= E0 - 0.01
  with the worst 14-day R_liq at least 2 points better or CS_HIT at least 0.02 higher.
- The neighbours **hold** if each passes the hard gates and scores at least its same-EMA reference - 0.03.
- **Propose** only if DC20 is better and both neighbours hold. Otherwise **keep the trend sleeve in down mode**.
- Report-only: hit rates by market type (strong falls are the target), median / mean and worst R_liq, the two real
  rounds, fees, and the lock-in numbers.
