# Pre-registration: split the money between two models (Baitoey, 2026-10-04)

Written and committed before any run. Not edited after results exist; results go to
`results/baitoey/20261004-mixes/` and a report committed under `research/baitoey/results/`.

## Question

An exploratory screen (`results/baitoey/20261004-counter-strategies.txt`, after looking at 29 mixes) found that half
the money in e20v45 and half in pol_abl_shock scored 0.587 with a worst round of -23.2%, against 0.568 / -26.2% for
e20v45 alone; mixes with mean-reversion models cut the worst round most but lowered the score. Do these mixes hold
on the full scoring (CS_HIT and the lock-in included)?

## Method

Two separate sub-accounts, no rebalancing between them: the mix's equity path is a w / 1 - w sum of each model's
cached v2 equity path (each normalised to 1 at the round start), its gross the equity-weighted gross. Scored with the
method of Pol's results/pol/20261002-v2check/endgame2.py (v2 weights and bars, V1 FLOORED composite on HKT days for
CS_HIT, his ~150-team cut as the top-20 proxy; the unmodified e20v45 path must reproduce its score file), without and
with the live lock-in applied to the whole account (+3% from day 10, exit at the next hour at 0.13%). Real-round
replays: the weighted mean of the two models' replay returns, ranked against last season's leaderboards.

| Mix | Models | Weights (first / second) |
|---|---|---|
| X1 | pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45 / pol_abl_shock | 0.4, **0.5**, 0.6 |
| X2 | pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45 / pol_mr_cap | 0.4, **0.5**, 0.6 |
| X3 | baitoey_vt_mom / pol_mr_cap | 0.4, **0.5**, 0.6 |

Base E0: e20v45 alone with the lock-in (the launch setup). Script: `research/baitoey/mixes.py`.

## Verdict (fixed now), per mix, all with the lock-in

- The 50/50 mix is **better** than E0 if HEADLINE_RET >= E0 + 0.03, or HEADLINE_RET >= E0 - 0.01 with the worst
  14-day R_liq at least 2 points better or CS_HIT at least 0.02 higher.
- Its neighbours (40/60 and 60/40) **hold** if HEADLINE_RET >= E0 - 0.03.
- **Propose the mix** only if the 50/50 mix is better and both neighbours hold; if several pass, report them all for
  the team to choose. Otherwise **keep e20v45 alone**.
- Report-only: the same numbers without the lock-in, the ~150-team cut share, mean R_liq, the real-round replays.

## Known limits, stated before running

- X1 was found after looking at 29 mixes; the neighbours and the fixed verdict limit that, not remove it.
- Separate sub-accounts are an approximation of one bot holding both books (orders would net; fees slightly lower).
- The hard gates are not recomputed for a mix; each component passes them.
- The post-holdout windows (Jul-Sep 2026) are in the pool and were already seen.
- The round starts 2026-10-04 12:00 UTC; nothing here changes the launch. A positive result would need new live code
  (two books in one account), the team rule for upgrades and the team agreement before the 2026-10-10 freeze.
