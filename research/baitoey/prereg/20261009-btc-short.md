# Pre-registration: baitoey_btc_short, a BTC-only short sleeve (Baitoey, 2026-10-09)

Written and committed before any code runs on data; not edited after results; no tuning after results.
Research for the team; nothing here changes the live bot. Results: `results/baitoey/20261009-btc-short/`; the report
is committed under `research/baitoey/results/`.

## Common rules (all variants)

- BTCUSDT only. The book is either cash or BTC short at 40% of equity; never long.
- "Daily close" = the 16:00 UTC hourly close (the decision hour). Entries and signal covers are decided there; the
  engine fills at the next hour's close. Stops are checked on every hourly close.
- EMA40 = EMA of hourly closes with span 40 x 24 (as team_trend_2). Daily statistics (20-day low / mean, 10-day
  high, 20-day return) use the previous daily closes, not today's.
- Entry price = the close of the fill hour (the hour after the decision).
- Hard stop: an hourly close at or above entry x 1.045 closes the short. Time stop: 168 hours after entry.
- Events: no new short when a CPI or FOMC_MINUTES time of `validation/event_calendar_v1.csv` lies within the 12 hours
  after the decision (the file has no FOMC rate-decision rows). Open shorts are kept.
- After any exit, a new short needs a fresh signal (A, D: a daily close that does not meet the entry condition since
  the exit; B: a close below the 20-day low on a later day than the exit; C: a new touch and failure after the exit).
- The state is rebuilt at every call from the last 90 days of hourly closes, starting flat (a pure model).
- Spec band 0.10 (no drift trades on a single position); decisions every hour.

| Variant | Model | Entry at a daily close | Cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| A trend | baitoey_btc_short_a | close < EMA40 x 0.97 and 20-day return < 0 | daily close > EMA40 x 1.03, or the stops |
| B breakdown | baitoey_btc_short_b | close < lowest of the previous 20 daily closes | daily close > highest of the previous 10 daily closes, or the stops |
| C failed rally | baitoey_btc_short_c | close < EMA40, close < mean of the previous 20 daily closes, and some hourly close in the previous 72 hours at or above that mean (the touch) | an hourly close at or above the bounce high (max hourly close of those 72 h) x 1.005, or the stops |
| D trend + funding | baitoey_btc_short_d | A, and BTC funding (72-hour mean, Pol's read-only table data/external/binance_futures_ext/funding8h_mean72.parquet) at or above its trailing 365-day 10th percentile | as A |

D's "double-check entries when funding is above its 90th percentile and OI is rising" is **not implemented**: open
interest history starts 2025-08-31 (cannot be tested on 2020-2026) and "double-check" has no mechanical meaning.

## Add-on to pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45 (baitoey_e20_btcs_a / _b / _c / _d)

e20v45 as registered, plus the variant's short, only while e20v45's own switch (BTC 20-day EMA, +-3%) reads down at
the daily decision: an entry needs the switch down; a switch reading up at a daily decision closes the short. While
the short is open, e20v45's book is scaled to at most 60% gross (total <= 100%). Between daily decisions the e20v45
part is kept; only the short's stops act.

## Scoring and reading

Each model: `backtest.run --model <name> --score2` (Book's weak-market board) and its score.json over all 2,298
windows. Reported: weak-case and all-window median, 10th and 90th percentile return; HEADLINE_RET, REL SCREEN /
CONFIRM, gates G1-G6, turnover and fees; from the model's own replay over the whole history: trades, hit rate (after
0.3% round-trip costs), worst trade, squeeze rate (an hourly close at or above entry x 1.05 within 72 hours of entry).

- Standalone variants: report only.
- Add-ons: **better than e20v45** if the archived 228 h median and 10th percentile are both at least e20v45's, REL
  SCREEN and CONFIRM each at least e20v45's minus 0.05, and no hard gate fails. Otherwise not better.

## Known limits, stated before running

- Specified during a falling market; history is spent research. On 2026-10-09 11:00 UTC BTC was 2.1% above its EMA40
  (A, C, D inactive) and 2.0% above its 20-day low (B close to triggering).
- Funding is Binance perpetual funding; Roostoo has none. The 90-day replay can differ from a longer one in rare
  chains of back-to-back shorts.
