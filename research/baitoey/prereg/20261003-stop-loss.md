# Pre-registration: a per-coin stop-loss on e20v45? (Baitoey, 2026-10-03)

Written and committed before any run. Not edited after results exist; results go to
`results/baitoey/20261003-stop-loss/` and a report committed under `research/baitoey/results/`.

## Question

On the first test-account run of `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45` (2 Oct 15:00 UTC to 3 Oct 07:00 UTC) the book fell 3.4%,
mostly from PUMP (-9%). Should the bot cut a coin that falls a set amount instead of holding it to the next daily
decision? Roostoo has no stop orders, so the stop is bot logic checked every hour.

## Rule tested

`src/models/baitoey/_stop_loss.py`: the daily model decides at 16:00 UTC as usual. At every other hour, a coin held
long that has fallen at least `stop` since the 16:00 UTC bar (a short: risen at least `stop`) is closed to cash and
stays out until the next daily decision. If the book is empty between decisions (first hour of a round, or every
coin stopped), the daily model decides afresh and leaves out the coins past the stop (the same handling as the
crash brake `pol_abl_shock`).

## Verdict (fixed now)

- A candidate is **better** if it passes the hard gates, HEADLINE_RET >= E0 - 0.01 and >= C0 - 0.01, and either
  the worst 14-day R_liq improves on E0 by at least 2 points or CS_HIT rises by at least 0.02.
- A candidate is **worse** if it fails a hard gate or HEADLINE_RET < E0 - 0.03 (the team margin).
- Otherwise **no clear difference**.
- **Propose to the team** only if both S5 and S10 are better (no picking the best level).
- Report-only: HEADLINE_RET without the post-holdout windows, CS_HIT, hit rates in strong / mild falls and rises and
  in volatile windows, weighted median and mean R_liq, worst R_liq, the two real-round replays, fees, and against C0
  the share of rounds the stop changed (by more than 0.1%), helped and hurt, with the mean change in those rounds.

## Known limits, stated before running

- The stop is measured from the 16:00 UTC bar, not from each fill price (the daily decision resets the book then).
- The post-holdout windows (Jul-Sep 2026) are in the pool and were already seen.
- The round starts 2026-10-04 12:00 UTC; nothing here changes the launch. A positive result would go through the
  team rule for upgrades (pre-written rule plus three days of paper) like any other change.
- An hourly rule needs the live guard fix for hourly models (commit 149d564) before it could run live.
