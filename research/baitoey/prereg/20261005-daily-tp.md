# Pre-registration: a daily take-profit on e20v45 (Baitoey, 2026-10-05)

Written and committed before any run. Not edited after results exist; results go to `results/baitoey/20261005-daily-tp/`
and a report committed under `research/baitoey/results/`.

## Question

Buy at the daily decision (16:00 UTC = 00:00 HKT) as usual; during the day, once the account is up X% on its value at
that decision, sell everything and hold cash until the next decision, then buy the model's book again. Does it beat
e20v45 as launched (with the lock-in)?

## Method

On e20v45's cached v2 paths (2,298 windows; the unmodified path must reproduce its score file). Each window splits into
cycles at its start (12:00 UTC) and at every 16:00 UTC (hours 0, 4, 28, 52, ...). Within a cycle the account follows the
model's hourly path; checked at each hour close, once the account is X above its value at the cycle start, it exits at
the next hour (0.13% on the gross held) and holds cash to the cycle's end; at the next cycle start it buys the model's
book back (0.13% on the gross) and follows the path again. The live lock-in (+3% on the round start from day 10, cash
to the end) applies on top. Scored by the method of Pol's endgame2.py: HEADLINE_RET, CS_HIT, Pol's ~150-team cut,
mean and worst R_liq. Script: `research/baitoey/daily_tp.py`.

| Run | Rule |
|---|---|
| L0 | e20v45 + lock-in (as launched) |
| TP05 / TP10 | daily take-profit at +0.5% / +1%, plus the lock-in |
| TP20 | daily take-profit at +2%, plus the lock-in (report-only) |

## Verdict (fixed now)

A run is **better** if HEADLINE_RET >= L0 + 0.03, or HEADLINE_RET >= L0 - 0.01 with CS_HIT at least 0.02 higher or the
worst round at least 2 points better; and its top-20 proxy is not lower than L0's. **Propose** only if TP05 and TP10 are
both better. Otherwise **keep the strategy as launched**. Report-only: TP20, how often the take-profit fires per round.

## Known limits, stated before running

- Re-entering at the next decision takes the model's book at that hour (its own path continues), an approximation.
- Hourly checks only (the live bot runs hourly). The round is live; nothing here changes the bot.
