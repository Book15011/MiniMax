## The overlays (Baitoey's spec, with the details fixed here)

All decisions use only bars closed at or before the decision; a decision at bar t fills at t + 1 h (the engine's lag).
"Daily close" = the 16:00 UTC hourly close (the models' decision hour). Shorts chosen by the base model are left as
they are; no overlay ever opens a short ("shorts off" is read as: the overlay itself adds no shorts).

1. **alt_vs_btc_switch.** At each daily decision: alt basket 10-day return = the mean simple return over the last
   240 hours of every coin in the point-in-time universe other than BTCUSDT that has both prices; BTC 10-day return
   the same for BTCUSDT. If the basket's return is below BTC's, the base model's long weights on non-BTC coins are
   moved to BTCUSDT (variant **a**) or to cash (variant **b**). Otherwise the base targets are unchanged.
2. **breakdown_exit.** A coin is *blocked* from a daily close below the lowest of its previous 20 daily closes until
   a later daily close above the mean of its previous 20 daily closes (state rebuilt from the last 120 days of
   daily closes at every decision). The base model's long weight on a blocked coin is set to 0 and left in cash.
3. **event_derisk.** Events: every row of `validation/event_calendar_v1.csv` with event CPI or FOMC_MINUTES (the
   file has no FOMC rate-decision rows; sha256 cc2689945a2d2bd0...). Holdings that fill in [T - 12 h, T + 6 h) for
   any event time T are the base targets x 0.5 (gross halved, longs and shorts alike). The wrapper decides every
   hour: at 16:00 UTC (and at a window's first decision) the base model decides; at other hours the standing base
   book is kept, and the factor of the fill hour applied. **Control:** the same hourly wrapper with factor 1 (an
   hourly wrapper rebalances drifted weights beyond the band more often than the 24 h base; the control isolates
   the event effect from that).

The base models see the overlay's previous targets as their previous decision (as the live bot would).

## Scoring

Each model: `python -m backtest.run --model <name> --score2` (Book's weak-market board, protocol
603da2948b68ee0f5c8a, merged into this branch unchanged). Reported for each model and both bases:

- Weak-market cases (the board's frozen archived and fresh-BTC profiles, 25 cases each): median, 10th and 90th
  percentile return at 216, 228 and 240 hours from cash, liquidation-adjusted (as the board computes it); the
  board's rank and conservative score.
- REL: score.json period_check SCREEN and CONFIRM (Pol's period REL), and HEADLINE_RET.
- Gates G1-G6 (pass/fail and detail), eligibility.
- Turnover and fees: score.json activity mean_turnover, mean_fees_e0 + mean_spread_e0 (per 14-day window).

## Reading rule (fixed now)

An overlay is **better than its base** if all hold: on the archived profile at 228 h its median and 10th percentile
are both at least the base's; its SCREEN and CONFIRM REL are each at least the base's minus 0.05; and it fails no
hard gate the base passes. For event_derisk the same test is also made against its control. Otherwise it is
**not better**. The board's own conservative score is reported as is; no new statistics are added after results.

## Known limits, stated before running

- The weak cases are 25 per profile from about 16 quarters; small differences are noise.
- The overlays were specified with knowledge of the current market (a weak altcoin tape); the history is spent
  research, not a holdout.
- The event calendar is a fixed table read at import (CPI and FOMC minutes only).
- Roostoo execution and the live account are not modelled beyond the engine's usual costs.
