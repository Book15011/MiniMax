# Pre-registration: a mid-frequency (MFT) volume-spike strategy against e20v45 (Baitoey, 2026-10-05)

Written and committed before any run. Not edited after results exist; results go to `results/baitoey/20261005-mft/`
and a report committed under `research/baitoey/results/`.

## Question

The exploratory 15-minute screen (`results/baitoey/20261005-mft-screen.txt`, after looking) found the best gross lead
in "15-minute quote volume at least 3x its 24-hour average with the price up": +0.09% over the next hour and +0.11%
over 4 hours, against a round-trip cost of about 0.25%. Does a full long-only MFT strategy on that signal beat e20v45?

## Strategy (directional, long only; not HFT; about a few trades an hour at most)

- Universe: the point-in-time Roostoo universe of each day with 1-minute Binance data.
- Every 15 minutes (bar close): signal = that 15-minute quote volume >= K x its 24-hour average (96 bars) and a
  positive 15-minute return.
- Up to 5 positions, 20% of current equity each, filled in order of the 15-minute return; a coin already held is not
  bought again. Each position is sold after H hours.
- Costs: 0.1% + the coin's half-spread on each side (market). Limit-entry variant: a buy limit at the bid for 2 minutes
  (fills if a 1-minute low trades below it, 0.05% maker fee), otherwise market at minute 2 (the move counts).
- Continuous simulation; each 14-day round's return is the equity change from its 12:00 UTC start.

| Run | K | H | Entry | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M4 | 3x | 4 h | market | primary |
| M1 | 3x | 1 h | market | neighbour |
| M4K5 | 5x | 4 h | market | neighbour |
| M4L | 3x | 4 h | limit first (2 min) | report-only |

## Comparison and verdict (fixed now)

The same 14-day rounds (starts 2025-09-02 to 2026-09-15, 12:00 UTC) as e20v45's registered run: its per-round R_liq,
the v2 weights and bars of those rounds, and Pol's ~150-team cut (top-20 proxy).
- **Candidate** only if M4 clears the bars (mean of the three, weighted) at least 0.03 more often than e20v45 on these
  rounds, its top-20 proxy is not lower, and M1 and M4K5 both have a positive weighted mean return after costs.
- Otherwise **no candidate**. Report-only: M4L, trades and fees per round, mean / median / worst R, share positive.

## Known limits, stated before running

- One year of minute data; the screen that suggested the signal used the same year.
- Positions carry across round starts (a continuous account, not a fresh start each round).
- Roostoo's limit-order matching is UNVERIFIED (Binance 1-minute lows stand in).
- Live it would need a minute-level bot (new code, Pol's area), within 20 API calls a minute and no auto-retry; the
  round is live and the 2026-10-10 freeze applies. Nothing here changes the live bot.
