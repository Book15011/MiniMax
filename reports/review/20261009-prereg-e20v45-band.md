# Pre-registration: a lower trigger for e20v45's switch to its long/short trend mode (2026-10-09, Pol)

Written and committed **before** the model files and before any run. Asked by Pol: "use a lower bar to trigger the
switch to the short strategy".

## Today's rule

e20v45 decides once a day (16:00 UTC) between two modes:
- **Up:** momentum (vt_mom, 4.5% vol cap).
- **Down:** `pol_trend_ls`, long/short trend.

The switch uses BTC's 20-day EMA with a symmetric band of 3% (`hysteresis: 0.03`):
- it goes down below EMA × 0.97;
- it goes back up above EMA × 1.03;
- it stays put in between.

On 2026-10-08 14:00 UTC, BTC sat 1.4% under its EMA, inside the band, so the model stayed in momentum.

## Variants (one setting each; config only, no shared code changes)

| Name | `hysteresis` | Down below | Up above |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45_h2` | 0.02 | EMA × 0.98 | EMA × 1.02 |
| `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45_h1` | 0.01 | EMA × 0.99 | EMA × 1.01 |
| `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45_h0` | 0.00 | the EMA | the EMA |

Everything else is e20v45's.

## Test (fixed now)

**Machinery:** as in `20261009-prereg-e20v45-dual.md`:
- the 2,298 windows, tool `3036a0d8bfdaa6a0`;
- the lock-in on both sides;
- month bootstrap, 2,000 draws.

**A variant is adoptable only if all three hold,** against the live e20v45 + lock-in:

| Check | Requirement |
|---|---|
| HEADLINE_RET | ≥ +0.03, with the 90% lower bound > 0 |
| Top-20 proxy | Not lower |
| Worst 14 days | No worse by more than 2 points |

- **Three variants are tested,** so the interval is the per-variant interval; picking the best of three adds selection
  luck.
- **If more than one passes,** take the highest HEADLINE_RET and say it was the best of three.
- **If none passes,** the band stays at 3%.

**Reported, not decided on:**
- the risk score, rising and falling windows, the last 12 months, Round 1 and Final;
- the share of days in down mode and the number of switches per 14 days (whipsaw);
- Book's 25 + 25 weak-market cases (228 h from cash);
- which mode each variant would be in at the latest bar;
- the switch's turnover from the current paper holdings. The live account was not read: the AWS session had expired.

## Addendum 1 (2026-10-09 12:25 UTC): the switch into the short mode only, written before these variants exist or run

**Written after seeing the results above:** all three symmetric bands failed (`results/pol/20261009-band/compare_band.out`).
Pol asked for a lower bar to switch **into** the long/short mode. The symmetric bands also lowered the bar to switch
back to momentum. This addendum tests the literal request: only the down bar moves, the up bar stays at EMA × 1.03.

| Name | Down below | Up above |
|---|---|---|
| `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45_d2` | EMA × 0.98 | EMA × 1.03 (as live) |
| `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45_d1` | EMA × 0.99 | EMA × 1.03 |
| `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45_d0` | the EMA | EMA × 1.03 |

- **Code:** one helper, `src/models/pol/_switch_asym.py`: `_combo.btc_state` with separate down and up bars
  (`down_hysteresis`, `hysteresis`). With equal bars it gives `_combo.btc_state`'s state (tested). `_combo.py` is
  unchanged.
- **Same machinery, same adoption rule, same reported items** as above.
- **Six variants have now been tested,** three of them after a look at the first three. The per-variant interval
  understates the selection luck; a pass would need a margin, not a near miss.
