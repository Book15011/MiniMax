## 2. One structural problem: the tie group is decided by correlation, not by closeness

The pick is "the best CS_HIT inside the top model's tie group", and a model is tied when the 90% month-bootstrap
interval of its HEADLINE_RET difference to the top contains 0. That interval is wide for a model that moves
differently from the top and narrow for one that moves with it. So:

| Model | HEADLINE_RET | Difference to vt_mom [90%] | Tied? | CS_HIT | Pick order |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baitoey_vt_mom (top) | **0.609** | | yes | 0.368 | 2 |
| pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45 | 0.568 | -0.041 [-0.083, -0.002] | **no** (moves with vt_mom) | 0.384 | 6 |
| pol_switch_vt_tl | 0.548 | -0.061 [-0.136, +0.005] | yes | 0.366 | 3 |
| **pol_mr_cap** | 0.509 | **-0.100** [-0.192, +0.002] | **yes** (moves differently) | **0.414** | **1 = the pick** |

Adding `pol_mr_cap` (my model, and a fragile peak: entry thresholds 0.8 / 1.2 / 1.5 lose 29-47% of its v1 score) turns
the full-pool pick from vt_mom into a model 0.10 lower on the primary return score. The second-best return score
(0.568) is excluded from the group for being similar to the top. The pick also flips with small bar changes:

| Bars | Full pool | Without the post-holdout windows |
|---|---|---|
| Book's | pol_mr_cap | baitoey_mr_4h |
| +0.1% tolerance on every bar | baitoey_vt_mom | baitoey_mr_4h |
| LENIENT DOWN = 0% (~150-team cut) | baitoey_vt_mom | pol_mr_cap |
| both | baitoey_vt_mom | pol_mr_cap |

**Suggested fix (Book's call, a change to his pre-registration):** keep the bootstrap, but tie a model to the top
only if its point difference is also within a fixed margin (e.g. 0.03 HEADLINE_RET), so the risk tie-break only
separates models whose return scores are actually close. Checked (`v2_margin.py`, research rows `pol_sens_*` and
`pol_abl_*` never candidates): with a 0.03 margin all eight cells (4 bar settings x 2 pools) pick `baitoey_vt_mom`.

Smaller: with no tolerance, keep-alive noise earns hits around 0% (CASH 0.372 -> 0.152 with +0.1%, and
baitoey_mr_bbrsi leaves the tie group); the LENIENT bar is the #20 of a ~50-team field (earlier review).
