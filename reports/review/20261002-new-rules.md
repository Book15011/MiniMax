# New rules tested for the launch (2026-10-02, Pol), all on scoring v2 (tool 3036a0d8bfdaa6a0)

Exploratory: each rule's settings were fixed before its run, but the rules were chosen after the earlier results,
so treat gains as leads for the team, not as pre-registered picks. Scripts: `results/pol/20261002-v2check/`
(`endgame.py`, `endgame2.py`, `run_abl.py`, `replays_v2.py`).

## 1. End-of-round lock-in (new; implemented in the live runner, OFF by default)

Rule: from day 10 of the 14-day round, once the round's return reaches +X, sell everything at the next hour (taker
fee + spread) and hold cash to the end. Tested on each model's cached v2 equity paths (the unmodified paths reproduce
the score files exactly), scored with v2's bars, weights and risk composite. Grid fixed before running: X in
{3, 5, 8, 10}% x start day 10 or 12.

| Model | HEADLINE_RET change, lock +3% from day 10 [90% month bootstrap] | CS_HIT | Mean 14-day R | ~150-team cut HIT | q83-of-six HIT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baitoey_vt_mom | **+0.028** [+0.003, +0.053] | 0.368 -> 0.433 | +4.86% -> +3.96% | +0.037 | -0.013 |
| pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45 | **+0.040** [+0.017, +0.063] | 0.384 -> 0.470 | +4.84% -> +3.99% | +0.025 | -0.016 |
| pol_switch_vt_tl | **+0.026** [+0.003, +0.051] | 0.366 -> 0.451 | +3.88% -> +3.21% | +0.026 | -0.012 |
| baitoey_mr_4h | **+0.018** [+0.006, +0.034] | 0.461 -> 0.482 | +0.08% -> -0.04% | +0.011 | +0.009 |

- It locks a lead against a late reversal and makes the last days flat, so the risk score (the second stage of the
  real ranking) improves a lot. The cost is ~0.9% of mean return per round and, on the q83 bar, slightly fewer hits.
- Higher thresholds (5-10%) or a later start (day 12) give small or negative changes; the best cell is at the corner
  of the grid, partly because bars near 0% reward any early lock. Read the size as approximate.
- In a strong rally where the real top-20 cut is ~+8%, a lock at +3% gives that up; the ~150-team cut column (+0.6%
  in falling windows, best of six benchmarks in rising ones) still improves, so on our best estimate it helps.
- **Live:** `live.endgame` in config.yaml (feature/volume 2bb3b15), `enabled: false`. From day 10 after the round's
  first hour, if equity is >= 3% above that hour's equity, the bot sells everything and holds cash; the lock survives
  restarts, the exit is retried until out, and the guard keeps later days active. Tests in tests/test_live.py.
  Turning it on is one committed line, a team decision; it is a pre-coded rule, not a manual override.

## 2. Crash rule (BTC falls 5% within 4 hours -> cash for 24 hours; triggers on 2.8% of days)

| Model | without the rule | with the rule |
|---|---|---|
| baitoey_vt_mom | 0.609, worst 14-day -30.8% | 0.607, **worst -26.8%**; Final replay +11.3% (#2) |
| vt_mom with 4.5%/day vol cap | 0.616, worst -35.9% | 0.615, **worst -32.8%**; Final +13.4% (#2) |
| pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45 | 0.568, worst -26.1% | 0.569, worst **-30.9% (worse)** |

Helps the pure momentum model's tail at no cost in score; does not help the switch, which already leaves in
downtrends (the forced cash and re-entry fight its own exit). Use it only with the vt_mom family.

## 3. Vol cap 4.5%/day on vt_mom (from the sensitivity check)

-35.9% (deeper). With the crash rule: 0.615 and worst -32.8%.
