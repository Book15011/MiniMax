## 1. Where volume comes from

| Source | What it has | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Roostoo `/v3/ticker` | `CoinTradeValue` / `UnitTradeValue` = **Binance's rolling 24 h base / quote volume, exactly** (ratio 1.0000 on all 86 shared pairs, 3 snapshots; `Change` within 0.6 bps of Binance's 24 h change). No OHLCV, no history, nothing about trading on Roostoo itself | Live fallback for the newest hour (section 4) |
| Binance Vision (Data Sources Pack #1, what we use) | Klines with volume, quote volume, **trade count and taker-buy volume** (1m and 1h archives), trades, aggTrades | Research panel; live archive fallback |
| Binance REST klines | Same fields, live | Live primary (blocked on the research server; EC2 unverified) |
| CryptoDataDownload (pack #2, free) | Multi-exchange CSVs (Coinbase, Kraken, ...), breadth, funding | Not used: Roostoo prices are Binance's, so other exchanges' volume is a second-order signal |
| CoinAPI (pack #3, paid, free credits) | 400+ exchanges, L2/L3 books | Not needed for hourly decisions |

## 2. What volume tells us (2021-2026, point in time, nothing fitted)

Universe: the research panel's daily top 30. Rank ICs are cross-sectional Spearman correlations with the next 24 h
return (2,095 days); baskets are the top 6 coins, equal weight, gross of costs.

| Reading | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Taker-buy share** (order flow) vs its 30-day norm, 4 h / 24 h / 7 d | IC +0.002 to +0.005, t < 1.2; partial IC (after momentum, size, volatility) ~0 | **No signal.** Not worth adding taker-buy to the model view (a contract change) |
| **Abnormal coin volume** (24 h vs 7-day normal) | IC -0.018 (t -3.7): loud coins do slightly worse next day, by rank | Weak |
| **Momentum x volume**, by rank | Moves on heavy volume reverse (IC of the 24 h return -0.037, t -5.3); quiet moves persist at 72 h (+0.030, positive in all 6 years) | Real in the median... |
| **Momentum x volume**, by mean return | ...but the momentum basket's excess return **comes from the loud coins**: top-6 momentum +25 bps/day over the universe (t 4.8); restricted to below-median volume only +3 bps (t 0.9) | **A volume filter on momentum would cut PnL.** The fat right tail (pumps) comes with volume. Supports Baitoey's choice to leave `no_chase` off |
| **Liquidity** (30-day volume, Amihud) | Liquid coins have better median outcomes (IC +0.034 / -0.056), but momentum on the 15 most liquid coins earns less (Sharpe 1.11 vs 1.44 on all 30) | Don't shrink the universe |
| **Market-wide volume** (universe 24 h vs 30-day normal) | Fixed thresholds: next-day universe return -14 bps after quiet days (< 0.85) vs +51 bps after heavy days (> 1.15, t 2.7). But by year it flips: 2022 and 2026 inverted (2026: BTC -65 bps after heavy days) | Weak, unstable; scored below as `pol_vt_mvr` |
| **Our own trading volume** (past leaderboards, numbers only) | More trading, lower return in every round: Spearman(turnover, return) -0.50 / -0.65 / -0.59; median return of the most-trading quarter -4.0% / -11.0% / -5.0% vs the least-trading -0.1% / -0.5% / +6.9% | Our ~2.6x capital per 10 days is in the low, better group. Keep it |

## 4. Live bot: volume for ticker-only hours (fixed)

When Binance REST is unreachable (research server; EC2 unverified) and the archive has not published the day yet,
the newest hours came from the Roostoo ticker with price only. The paper bots had **16 of the last 36 hours without
volume**, so `baitoey_vt_mom`'s volume-confirmed exit (part of the launch pick) could not fire on recent hours, and
paper trading differed from the backtest. Its effect is modest (Baitoey's study: REL 1.717 with the rule vs 1.652
without, inside the noise), but the bot should behave as tested.

Fix (`src/live/feed.py: fill_ticker_volume`, runner passes `UnitTradeValue`): the ticker-only bar gets the ticker's
24 h quote volume minus the 23 bars before it, so the 24 h sum the models read equals Binance's figure exactly; with a
hole in those bars it gets 1/24 of it; never negative; the archive or REST replaces it later. The universe still ranks
only on complete source-backed data. Test: `test_ticker_volume_makes_the_24h_sum_match_the_tickers_figure`.
