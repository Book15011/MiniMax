# Flaw review before the round: what could still hurt us, and what changed (2026-10-03, Pol)

Scope: the live bot on EC2, the competition rules as the bot meets them, and the tests behind the launch pick.
Launch model unchanged: `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45`. Commit: the one after 5634856 on `feature/launch-e20v45`.

## Fixed

| # | Flaw | What could happen | Fix | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The service gave up after 10 restarts in 10 minutes | The bot reads Roostoo's exchangeInfo at startup. A Roostoo outage of about 5 minutes during a restart (an EC2 reboot, a crash) would leave the bot **stopped for good**, with nobody watching overnight | `deploy/minimax-bot.service`: no start limit, one restart a minute. `go_live_round.sh` installs the committed unit before starting | systemd behaviour |
| 2 | The round's starting equity was taken at the first hour even with $0 in the wallet | If the competition account is funded a few minutes after 20:00 HKT, the baseline would be $0. From day 10, the lock-in check would divide by it, **failing every hour to the end** | The baseline is the first hour with money in the account; the lock-in skips until there is one | `test_late_funding_at_the_rounds_first_hour`: the guard buys the targets the next hour, and no error after day 10 |
| 3 | No end of round in the bot | After Oct 18 20:00 HKT (when the exchange liquidates) the bot would buy back in at the next decision or guard | `live.end_at: "2026-10-18 12:00"`: from then on it records only | `test_nothing_trades_after_end_at` |
| 4 | A test run's state could carry into the round (fixed earlier today, 5634856) | Positions, active days and the round start from the test account | The state is stamped with the account, and a state whose round began before `start_at` is refused | 3 tests |

## Adopted: the end-of-round lock-in (`live.endgame`, now ON)

**Rule:** from day 10 after the round's first funded hour, once equity is 3% above that hour's, sell everything and
hold cash to the end. The guard keeps every day active. It had been built and tested (20261002-new-rules.md) but left
off as a team decision.

**Robustness check** (`results/pol/20261003-ext/lock_sweep.py`): the grid widened around the chosen cell, on the v2
paths.

- **Kept at +3% from day 10, the setting reported before this check.** Lower thresholds score higher on
  HEADLINE_RET, partly because its 0% bars reward any early lock. They were found after looking, so they are not
  adopted.

## Checked, no change needed

- **The switch today:** BTC 84,872, 3.05% above its 20-day EMA, so the **up** state (momentum). It leaves only below
  ~79,900 (-5.9%). The round will almost surely start in momentum, where e20v45 and vt_mom are the same book.
- **Activity:**
  - Start at 20:00 HKT: the guard is already armed (hour 20 of the HKT day), so a first decision that fills nothing
    is retried the next hour.
  - Oct 18 (ends 20:00 HKT): the 00:00 HKT decision or the 12:00 HKT guard trades.
  - 15 HKT days in all.
- **The minimum of 8 active days** is reached by day 8, before the lock-in can start (day 10). Locked days stay active
  through the keep-alive trade.
- **Tokenized stocks:** not in our 65-pair universe list.
- **Rate limit at the start rush:** about 24 calls in the busiest hour. A slow or failed order is confirmed or retried
  from the account the next hour.
- **Public-repo methods** (20261003-ext-baselines.md on feature/ext): no rebuilt method or combination improves the
  launch model. Funding as a crowding filter adds nothing.

## Test-method notes (sharpened)

- **HEADLINE_RET's 0% bars favour any early lock**, so judge end-of-round rules on the ~150-team cut and the risk
  score as well. That is how the lock-in was checked above.
- **Every result now also lists the two real past-round replays** (Round 1, falling; Final, rising), in the
  ext-baselines and lock-in tables.
- **Market-type splits matter.** Rising-window cut losses can hide inside an overall gain (the lock-in, above).
