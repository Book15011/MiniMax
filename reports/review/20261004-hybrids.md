## 5. Caveats (UNVERIFIED)
- **The idea came from the same data.** The hybrids were proposed after part 4.2 of the E decision split E5's gap by
  market type over these same 2,298 windows. Their rules were pre-registered before any run, but the mechanism was not
  found independently of the data. This is an in-sample result; the live round is the first out-of-sample test.
- **The margin is small.** +0.009 HEADLINE_RET overall; SCREEN +0.0015. Only views (a) and (e) separate from zero.
- **The 14-day direction is not predictable** (`docs/EVALUATION.md`). Breadth here only decides whether to stage. That it
  will keep separating rising from falling markets live is UNVERIFIED.
- **H1 has not run in shadow mode.** The two shadow bots run vt_mom and E5, as set up earlier; they were left
  untouched.

## 6. Recommendation
**H1 ("E-breadth") passed as a CANDIDATE and is registered.** Treat it as the team's candidate alongside vt_mom, not as
proven better: its edge is small and was found on the same data. A shadow bot for H1 before any switch would add
