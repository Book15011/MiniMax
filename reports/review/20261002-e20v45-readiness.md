## Shorting is not needed

Over all 2,298 windows (v2), the same strategy with every short refused (the bot's automatic fallback if the exchange
says no):

| | HEADLINE_RET | Hit at a ~150-team top-20 cut | Mean 14-day R | Worst 14 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| with shorts | 0.568 | 0.365 | +4.84% | -26.2% |
| **long-only** | **0.575** | 0.361 (-0.004 [-0.052, +0.040]) | **+5.14%** | **-25.4%** |

Its edge comes from cutting exposure in downtrends while keeping the coins still trending up, not from betting on
falls (shorts help only inside falling windows: -2.3% vs -2.8% there). Running it long-only removes the one function
we cannot test before the round, at no cost.
