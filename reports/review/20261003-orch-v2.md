**In one line:** the forecaster ranks coins better than chance (cross-sectional IC +0.047, t 14.9), but there is no tradable return in it at 8 hours after costs. Its confident calls fail the cost bar (FALL calls are followed by *rising* prices on average), and its shuffled control is not neutral. So F and FS were never scored, and neither R nor E beats A′. Keep `baitoey_vt_mom` at its own band.

## 1. Phase 1: the forecaster and its skill gate

**Data and training.**
- 1,012,644 coin-hours (the liquid 20, from 2020-06-01 16:00), 37 features.
- 278 weekly retrains (2021-06-07 to 2026-09-28) × 3 horizons × L and S. That is 1,668 models per label set and 881,244 out-of-sample forecast rows.
- The stored forecasts equal a live recomputation from a truncated view: within 1e-12 on 6 random hours (test), and exactly at the card hour.
- 90.1% of A′'s long coin-hours after the first retrain have a forecast. The rest are base coins outside the liquid 20.

### 3.5 Shadow-log format (implemented and tested, not started)
`book_orch.coin_log(view, w, records, fc, p)` is pure and gives one record per coin per hour, for every coin that is held, targeted, forecast or decided on. `BookOrch.explain(view)` gives the targets and each coin's decision and reason. Example:

```json
{"coin": "AAVEUSDT", "hour": "2026-09-30T00:00:00+00:00",
 "signals": {"trend_score": 1.0, "sigma30": 0.00903, "broken_down": false, "in_universe": true, "liquid": false, "previous_target": 0.0},
 "probabilities": null, "decision": "enter", "reason": "base decision", "size": 0.111}
```

- For a liquid coin, `probabilities` holds P(rise/flat/fall) at 2/4/8 h, q, the horizon means, the base rates, E8 and the two confidence flags.
- Shadow mode would also need the live forecast path (`_orch_ml.live`, tested equal to the stored forecasts) wired into `targets()` for hours past the stored range. That is not done.

## 5. Tests
- `tests/test_book_orch.py` covers: layers off = the base held between decisions; R, E (staging, top-up, breakdown block), F (multiplier, filter, rescale, exit, re-entry, the base seeing our longs) and FS (sizes, ranking, limits, room, closes); no look-ahead; labels by hand; purge, S window and first retrain; shuffles; determinism and the run key; stored = live on real data.
- 15 tests. Full suite: **295 passed** with scikit-learn; without it 293 passed and 2 skipped.

## 6. Deviations and notes
- **No default changed.**
- **Dry run.** Before the real training, the full pipeline was run once on a 26-week retrain schedule (11 retrains) to check the code. Its gate-like numbers were seen. Nothing could be tuned on them, and nothing was changed.
- **Registration command (not needed).** `backtest.run --model … --score` also runs the old harness, whose cache is `data/harness/`. That conflicts with this task's read-only `data/`. Had a layer passed, I would have registered through `backtest.scoring.report.score_and_publish` (scoring cache in `results/`) and recorded it as a DEVIATION.
- **"Predictable" per coin** is read as the IC of daily means > 0 with t ≥ 2 (section 3.3).
- **K1's bootstrap label** is reported, not required (as pre-registered). Both layers are "tied".

## 7. Recommendation
**Launch `baitoey_vt_mom` alone at its own band (0.05).**
- No v2 layer beats it.
- The forecaster's coin ranking is statistically real but carries no return after costs at 8 hours.
- Whether it has value as a filter at longer horizons is UNVERIFIED and would need its own pre-registration.
