## Why it loses

The lower trigger fires after BTC has already fallen 1–3% below its 20-day average, often near a short-term low. In
both live-data episodes, a rebound followed:
- **Sep 15–17** (BTC at 0.98–0.99 × EMA): the lower triggers went long/short, shorting PUMP, TRUMP, SUI and PEPE. BTC
  was at 1.05 × EMA by Sep 18.
  - e20v45 stayed long: **$97k → $120k** between Sep 16 and Sep 19.
  - The lower triggers: **$97k → $100k**. They never caught up.
- **Oct 8 16:00** (BTC at 0.9717 × EMA, 0.2% above the live trigger): the lower triggers shorted ZEC (37% of the book)
  and bought a little BTC and SOL. BTC rose about 2% in the next 20 hours.
  - e20v45: **+2.8%**.
  - The lower triggers: **−2.8%**. The ZEC short alone cost $3.1k.

The extra trading costs little; the round's extra turnover is about $115k, roughly $115 in fees. The loss comes from
being short at the wrong time.
