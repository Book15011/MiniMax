# Organisers' statement on tick scalping (2026-10-05)

A public message from the organisers to all teams, pasted by Pol. It is verbatim apart from line breaks, and it names
no team.

> Hey folks, thanks to those who asked about this. It's thoughtful. Here is our view, which applies equally to all
> teams:
>
> 1. Strategies that place a limit buy at one price level and a limit sell one tick higher (tick scalping) are not
>    prohibited and won't be disqualified.
>
> 2. However, finalist selection is not based on leaderboard rank alone. Judges will assess whether each strategy
>    would actually hold up in real markets. Tick scalping without considering queues works in simulator but very
>    likely failed in real markets so this won't be considered as high value strategy.
>
> We encourage all teams to focus on strategies whose edge comes from the market itself, not from the mechanics that
> can't be reproducible in real markets.

## Team reading

- **Allowed:** tick scalping is allowed, and won't cost a disqualification.
- **Low value to judges:** the judges will not count it as a high-value strategy.
- **The return gate is hard:** Pol was told on 2026-10-07 that the top-20 return ranking is a hard gate before
  judging. Hence the sleeve beside the main strategy (`src/live/sleeve.py`).
- **The main strategy stays the one we present.** The sleeve is declared separately.
