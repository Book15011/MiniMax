**On balance the stops sold before bounces, not before further falls.** Over the next 1 to 3 days, stopped coins
rose more often than they fell. Over 7 days it is a coin flip, with a positive mean.
- **What they bought:** a better worst window (−25.3% vs −30.8%) and a smaller drop-set median (−12.9% vs −18.1%).
- **What they cost:** 0.7% of E₀ per window in return, extra turnover (+25%), and much lower HIT in DOWN windows.
- **A likely reason (UNVERIFIED, not tested):** the stops fire in falling fortnights, where the coins A keeps often
  recover enough by the window's end to clear the −1.5% LENIENT bar.
- **The empty-book rule.** Every full stop-out stayed in cash until the next decision, as intended, and no window
  start was delayed. The weakness noted before the runs (the base refilling at once when it no longer likes the
  stopped coins) never occurred in the shared history.

| Rebound median R (K7) | +19.4% vs A +21.4% (**−2.0 pp**) | +20.7% (−0.7 pp) |

**Reading.**
- **The episodes are short.** Breadth crosses 0.3 back and forth, and each crossing opens or closes the short.
  Most episodes pay 0.2% plus the spread to hold BTC short for a few hours, so the average episode loses about the
  cost.
- **2022 is the only year the hedge made money** (40% win rate, +0.48% per episode).
- **HIT DOWN improves by only 0.006.** The hedge is too small and too short-lived to lift DOWN windows over the bar,
  and it gives back 2 pp in rebound windows.
- **Gross above 1 (UNVERIFIED as a rule breach).**
  - Targets never exceed 1, and the engine caps gross at 1 after every trade.
  - Between trades, a rising BTC price grows the short's loss while the longs drift. Exposure then reached up to
    101.9%, at some hour in 414 windows (BH) and 375 (TS+BH).
  - Whether the competition counts such drift as a breach is UNVERIFIED. A live hedge would need a small buffer or
    an hourly trim. BH is not kept, so nothing is changed.

- **Smoke results** (20 windows) were used for correctness only: replay = R, stops and hedges open and close, and
