## Reading

- **The filter does what it promises.** It cuts the downside in weak markets (the worst case is 3–4 points better),
  **but it costs upside everywhere else.** The full-history score falls by 0.016 and the top-20 chance by 0.023 (0.046
  over the last 12 months).
- **vt_dual on its own** also doesn't beat the live configuration with the lock-in: 0.606 vs 0.608, with a much lower
  top-20 proxy (0.306).
- **With the top-20 return cut as a hard gate, giving up top-20 chance for a smaller worst case is the wrong trade.**
- **The return engine for the rest of the round is the sleeve** (`feature/scalp-sleeve`). e20v45 stays as the
  presented strategy on its share.
