## 2. Execution: limit orders could save ~0.2% per 14 days (ready to test)

The pick pays 0.46% of capital in fees per 14-day window (turnover 4.64) plus 0.06% spread, all as a taker (0.1%).
Roostoo's maker fee is 0.05%. If passive limit orders fill, that is up to ~0.2% per window, which moves the pick's
chance of clearing a ~150-team top-20 cut from 23.2% to 24.6% of windows (`fee_effect.py`). Whether Roostoo's mock
matching fills a resting limit order, and how fast, is unknown. `python -m src.live.fillcheck --limit` (20a0a29) now
answers it on the TEST account: a LIMIT BUY at the bid, wait, cancel, sell back passively, end flat, report role, time
to fill, fee rate. **Run it once the test wallet is funded.** Only if fills are reliable is a maker-first mode worth
building (after launch; the bot stays on market orders).

- The **vol cap** is the cleanest improvement: one number, a structural reason (the top-20 cut pays for upside, so a

- **Both together** (`pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45`, registered, commit 014e8f8; scored twice, identical): best-bar REL
