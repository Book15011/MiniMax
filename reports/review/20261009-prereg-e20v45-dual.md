# Pre-registration: e20v45 with the dual-momentum filter (2026-10-09, Pol)

Written and committed **before** the model code exists on this branch and before any run.

## Why

Book's weak-market board (`feature/book-bear-eval` `9fb7b1f`) ranks `baitoey_vt_dual` first and e20v45 23rd over 25
historical weak-market starts like today:
- 9.5-day means of +2.95% vs +0.74%;
- the 7 cases where the fall continued: −3.3% vs −10.3%.

No model clears that board's own conservative bar. On the original full-history board (without the lock-in),
`baitoey_vt_dual` scores 0.587 vs e20v45's 0.568, with a lower top-20 proxy (0.351 vs 0.384).

The question here is narrower: **does adding vt_dual's absolute-momentum filter to e20v45's up-state sleeve improve
the live configuration?**

## The candidate (one setting, no tuning)

**`pol_switch_vtd_tl_e20v45`** = `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45` with one change:
- **Up-state sleeve:** `baitoey_vt_dual` (`min_score: 0.0`) instead of `baitoey_vt_mom`, with the same 4.5%/day vol
  cap.
- **Unchanged:** the BTC 20-day EMA ±3% switch and the down-state `pol_trend_ls` sleeve.
- **Code:** `baitoey_vt_dual` is copied unchanged from Baitoey's `feature/mom-ss` (`4ae9f1c`). Its dependencies
  (`baitoey_vt_mom`, `_volume_signals`, `_combo`, `contracts`) are identical on both branches.

## Test (fixed now)

**Machinery:** the same as `results/pol/20261003-ext/compare_h1.py`:
- tool `3036a0d8bfdaa6a0`;
- the 2,298 windows, the three return bars and the top-20 cut;
- month bootstrap, 2,000 draws;
- the end-of-round lock-in applied to both (+3% from day 10, as live).

**Adopt for the next update only if all three hold,** against `pol_switch_vt_tl_e20v45` + lock-in (the live
configuration):

| Check | Requirement |
|---|---|
| HEADLINE_RET | Ahead by **≥ +0.03**, with the paired 90% interval's lower bound **> 0** |
| Top-20 proxy | **Not lower** than the live configuration's (stricter than the usual −0.02 allowance, as agreed today) |
| Worst 14-day window | No worse by more than 2 points |

**Reported, not part of the decision:**
- the risk score;
- rising and falling windows;
- the last 12 months;
- the Round 1 and Final replays;
- the median drawdown;
- Book's 25 archive-profile weak-market cases: the mean, median, p10 and worst 228 h return from cash, recomputed
  from the same window runs with the first 228 hours and one liquidation. This is checked against his published
  e20v45 (+0.74%) and vt_dual (+2.95%) numbers.

**If it fails:** e20v45 stays unchanged, and the result is reported as is.
