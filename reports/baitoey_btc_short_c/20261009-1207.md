## How to read this

- Costs: 0.10% per side (long and short) plus each coin's half-spread; orders fill one bar after the decision.
- The engine forces a trade on any HKT day with none by hour 11 of the day.
- Composite A: daily returns, annualized ratios, Calmar on the annualized return. Composite B: hourly returns, annualized ratios, Calmar on the raw 14-day return. Both = 0.4 Sortino + 0.3 Sharpe + 0.3 Calmar; the official formula is published only on Finale Day.
- SCREEN / CONFIRM follow the validation split; windows ending after the holdout date are sealed until the launch model is frozen.
